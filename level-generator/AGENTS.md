# M6 level-generator scope

This is the published independent generator prototype. Private research and downloads stay in ignored .local/level-generator/ and are never site assets. Read README.md and ../docs/LEVEL_GENERATOR.md before changing the generator.

- User's current rules supersede historical M3–M5 instructions: maximum 12×12 top-level placement cells, no orphan rooms, every hallway NETWORK joins >=2 distinct rooms, adjacent hallway cells may join, rooms can share structural walls or combine in eligible pairs.
- All generated structural surfaces are orthogonal, including sky scenery and traps/features. No curves/slopes. Floors and ceilings are flat, including a uniform flat hallway/stair ceiling. Hidden triangulation diagonals are not diagonal architecture.
- There is no special donut-room path. General Features have room-relative masks and combinable trap/waterfall/column/relief flags in sector.flatDetail. Count is area-based (default512m²/feature, hard cap8/room), with an independent 20% coverage cap and legal-placement/AI reservations.
- Adjacent decision probability is HALF THE CURRENT PARENT probability, e.g. 1/8→1/16→1/32. Never reset probability on success or failure. Flags realize this probability directly; rejected size/category inheritance draws a different legal proposal, with constrained fallback recorded if necessary. One directed parent avoids cyclic redecay. Keep explicit value/probability/source/depth/provenance.
- Corridor/door openings stay in [4,12]m. Alcoves vary width, depth, floor offset and clear height; each connection must be a legal single step with full composite-headroom clearance. Preserve bounded alcove count/depth/area and rare repeat path.
- Strict structural blockout: floors/walls/ceilings/door leaves and off-world box sky scenes only. No fixtures, textures, trim, bevels or dressing. Solid red lava / solid green radiation use existing structural surfaces.
- Compile one welded oriented shell with two opposing incidences per edge, positive integrated volume, no diagonal point-contact leaks and axis-aligned faces. Do not loosen closure tolerances. Shared walls and corner junctions are structural collision geometry.
- Certify the COMPLETE declared safe domain: exclude explicit inaccessible/lethal features and radioactive standoff, erode by composite AABB +12mm, and require all rooms/hallways/alcoves/reachable features in one component. Exact LUT endpoints/costs/next hops and continuous live-engine sweeps are required. Never substitute a connected subset.
- The frozen v0.1.3-based controller/BVH adapter is not current game parity. Preserve engine/BASELINE.json original hashes/adaptation notes. Fixed WASM32MiB; current composite covers18 model allowances +level-pitch machinegun yaw, not all weapons/crowds/pitches.
- Generator/nav changes bump version and rebuild catalogue. Rust/controller changes also rebuild WASM. Schema/hash mismatch rejects stale packages; failed/superseded candidates retain active world.
- Doors open in0.25s with shared rendering/SAT transforms, all-Self no-crush occupancy and per-leaf start/finish sound. Preserve independent128 local sample /128 external graph-sector work limits, finite queues/lifetimes, six default graph transitions and <=4 wall taps. Dynamic aperture invalidates acoustic caches.
- Sky openings remain collision sealed for Selves/projectiles and absorb acoustic samples. Actual Features drive collision/damage; probability metadata never silently creates a hazard. Damage rules apply equally to player and AI.
- Record remaining production integration, natural/stacked/exterior variants, physical devices, other weapons and calibration limits accurately. Keep reports under ignored .local/level-generator-checks/.
